ENGINEERING MINDSET & AMBITION

Personal Narrative

I’m Vickey Chaudhary, a computer science student at Newton School of Technology (Ajeenkya DY Patil University, Pune) who believes that true engineering capability comes from building things yourself, not just watching tutorials.

My approach to software engineering is grounded in curiosity and first principles. Rather than relying solely on high-level abstractions, I strive to understand what happens under the hood—how Python manages data in memory, how algorithms scale with input size, and how well-architected software solves tangible everyday problems.

Currently, I spend my days writing modular Python scripts, analyzing algorithmic complexities, practicing version control on GitHub, and exploring modern frontend workflows. My goal is to build deep foundational mastery in computer science and grow into a full-stack engineer who builds resilient, high-impact systems.
